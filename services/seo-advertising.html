Measure useful work, then decide

Follow the path from an enquiry to a suitable job, a quote and paid work. Review unwanted enquiries and missed calls as well as the jobs won. A phone-link click alone does not show that a conversation or sale happened.

Start with a simple enquiry record. If advertising is worth trying, agree the campaign, spending limit and review point first. Use actual outcomes and available capacity to decide whether to continue.
