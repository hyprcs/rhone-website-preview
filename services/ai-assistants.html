AI assistants for everyday work

Give a helper a narrow, repeatable task, with clear information sources and a person reviewing important actions.

What it could help with

  • Prepare a daily list of enquiries or tasks that need attention.
  • Find information in approved business documents and show where the answer came from.
  • Draft a job summary or handover from supplied notes for someone to check.

A sensible first step

Pick one low-risk task and the information it may use. Test it with fictional or approved examples, agree the approval steps, and decide what should happen when information is missing.

Start small

Tailored quote after a short discussion

Sometimes called agentic automation. Access, judgement, reliability and review effort determine the scope. Start with one useful task and agree the quote before building.

A useful question

What could a helper prepare for you, while leaving the final decision with you?

These are possible projects to scope and test, not existing integrations. Customer messages, spending and business commitments need explicit agreed authority.
