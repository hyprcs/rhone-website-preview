n8n workflow setup

An n8n workflow is a set of steps you arrange for a computer to carry out automatically. n8n connects the apps involved so information can move between them.

When this happens → do these things

Imagine someone fills out Rhone’s website form saying their air conditioner has stopped working. A workflow could:

  1. Collect their details — name, phone number, address and problem.
  2. Save the enquiry in one place.
  3. Send an acknowledgement so the customer knows their request was received.
  4. Notify Sam with the details, ready for him to call them.

Once connected and set up, those steps could happen whenever someone submits the form.

The benefit: less copying information around and less chance of an enquiry getting missed.

What it could help with

  • Organise enquiries: keep new customer requests together, flag missing details and make it clear who needs a response.
  • Follow up quotes: check which quotes are still unanswered and prepare a follow-up message for Sam to review.
  • Organise finished-job notes: collect work completed, hours, parts and photos so the information is ready when Sam prepares the invoice.

A sensible first step

Walk through one real job, from the customer’s first call to getting paid. Find a repetitive admin task, check what the existing software already handles, then choose one useful improvement. Build a small trial, check that repeat submissions and errors are handled, and provide simple operating notes.
