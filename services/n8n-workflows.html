n8n workflow setup

Connect a few agreed steps between the tools you already use, so information gets to the right place.

What it could help with

  • Organise a new enquiry and flag the missing details.
  • Prepare a follow-up for quotes that still need a decision, using current status and human review.
  • Turn completed-job notes into a clear handover for preparing an invoice.

A sensible first step

Choose one input, one destination and one outcome. Check access to the tools, build a small trial, test duplicates and failures, then hand it over with simple operating notes.
